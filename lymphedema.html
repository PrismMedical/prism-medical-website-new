Common Questions

Lymphedema & LTA FAQ

What does the Lymphedema Treatment Act cover?
Since 2024, Medicare covers compression supplies for lymphedema, including daytime garments, nighttime garments and bandaging systems, when prescribed. We verify your specific benefit before ordering.
How long do custom garments take?
Custom garments take 10–14 business days in production before shipping. We build that into every order and tell you the expected arrival date up front.
Do you work with my lymphedema therapist?
Yes. We coordinate directly with Certified Lymphedema Therapists on measurements and documentation, so your garment matches your therapy plan.
