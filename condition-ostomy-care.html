Coverage

What coverage usually looks like

How it is covered

Ostomy supplies are covered under most plans with a clinician's order, and monthly quantity allowances apply. Many people receive fewer than their plan permits.

Stomas change, and that is normal: Size and shape shift over the first months and again over time. If your system stops fitting well, call us. We adjust the order and connect you with fit resources.

Common Questions

Living With an Ostomy questions

What if my pouching system stops fitting well?
Call us. We will work with you and your care team to adjust the order, and we can connect you with fit resources.
How many supplies can I get each month?
Plans set monthly allowances. We verify yours and make sure you are receiving what you are entitled to, which is often more than people are getting.
Will the packaging say what is inside?
No. Ostomy and urology shipments go out in discreet, unmarked packaging.
