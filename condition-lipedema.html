Conditions · Lipedema

Compression for lipedema, fitted and replaced on schedule

Lipedema is frequently mistaken for obesity or for lymphedema, and the compression needs are their own. Getting the right garment and replacing it on time is most of the management.

What We Supply

What we supply for lipedema

  • Flat knit compression garments sized to limb shape
  • Graduated compression for legs, arms and torso
  • Bandaging supplies for reduction phases
  • Donning aids for higher compression classes

What coverage usually looks like

How it is covered

Coverage for lipedema compression varies more than it does for lymphedema and depends heavily on documentation. Our team works with your clinician on the medical-necessity record before submitting anything.

Documentation decides this one: Lipedema claims are approved or denied largely on the strength of the clinical record. Call us before ordering and we will tell you what your plan is likely to require.
