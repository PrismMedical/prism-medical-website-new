For Patients

Your supplies, without the second job of managing them

Managing a health condition is work. Chasing supplies shouldn't be more of it. Here's exactly what happens when Prism handles your resupply.

How it works for patients

Your clinician sends the order

A prescription and supporting documentation come to us directly. You don't have to carry paperwork anywhere.

We verify your coverage

Before anything ships, we confirm what your plan covers and tell you what to expect. No surprise bills.

Supplies arrive on schedule

Shipments follow your refill cycle. Discreet packaging for personal supplies, always.

Refills continue automatically

We confirm before each shipment and track prescription renewals so nothing lapses.
