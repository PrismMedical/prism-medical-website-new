Common Questions

Wound Care FAQ

How often will my supplies arrive?
Shipments follow the refill schedule on your clinician's order, typically every 30 days. We confirm before each shipment so nothing arrives that you don't need.
What if my dressing type changes?
Call us or have your clinician update the order. We adjust the very next shipment. No restart, no gap.
Is wound care covered by insurance?
Most wound care supplies are covered when ordered by your clinician with supporting documentation. Our team verifies your specific coverage before anything ships.
