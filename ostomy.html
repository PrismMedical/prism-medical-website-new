Common Questions

Ostomy Supplies FAQ

How many supplies will insurance cover?
Most plans cover set quantities per month based on Medicare guidelines. We verify your allowable quantities and build your refill schedule around them.
What if my pouching system stops fitting well?
Stomas change over time, and that's normal. Call us and we'll work with you and your care team to adjust your order to a better-fitting system.
Is shipping discreet?
Yes. All ostomy supplies ship in plain, unmarked packaging.
