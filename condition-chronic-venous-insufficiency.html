How it is covered

Compression for venous disease is covered under many plans when prescribed, and the requirements differ from the lymphedema benefit. We check yours before ordering.

Replace every six months: Graduated compression loses its gradient as the fabric relaxes, usually within about six months of daily wear. We track your replacement schedule so the garment you are wearing is still doing its job.

Common Questions

Chronic Venous Insufficiency questions

How do I know what compression class I need?
Your clinician prescribes the class. We handle fitting, measurement support and verification from there.
What if I cannot get the stockings on?
Tell us. Donning aids solve most of it, and wrap systems are an option when they do not. This is one of the most common reasons compression gets abandoned, and it is fixable.
