Resources & FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most

Product-specific questions live on each product line page. These are the ones that apply to everyone.

How do I get started with Prism?
Ask your clinician to send your prescription to Prism, or call us at 888.244.6421 and we'll coordinate directly with your clinician's office, including requesting records if you're switching from another supplier.
Do you take my insurance?
We work with Medicare and a broad range of commercial plans. Coverage varies by product and plan, which is why we verify your specific benefits before your first shipment, and tell you exactly what to expect in plain language.
How do I pay my bill?
Through PersonaPay, our secure encrypted payment portal. We never request payment information by email. If something on your bill looks wrong, call us first. Most billing questions resolve in one call.
How do I track my order?
Your shipment confirmation includes carrier tracking. You can also call 888.244.6421 and we'll read you the live status. Custom garments spend 10–14 business days in production before shipping. We give you that window when you order.
Why is there no contact form or patient portal login on this site?
By design. Web forms and logins that touch health information create HIPAA exposure we've chosen to avoid entirely. This site collects no protected health information. Orders, questions and account matters are handled by phone and through secure channels like PersonaPay and your clinician's ordering portal.
What happens when my insurance changes?
Call us with your new plan details and we'll re-verify your coverage before your next shipment. Plan-year changes in January are the most common cause of supply interruptions anywhere. Telling us early is the best way to avoid a gap.
Can I get supplies from more than one product line?
Yes, and it's simpler with one supplier. Wound care, CGM, lymphedema, compression, ostomy and urology all run through the same account, the same phone number and the same verified-coverage process.
What is the Lymphedema Treatment Act, and does it apply to me?
Since 2024, Medicare covers compression supplies for lymphedema, including nighttime garments many patients were never told about. If you manage lymphedema, call us and we'll verify what your benefit actually includes. Details on our Lymphedema & LTA page.
