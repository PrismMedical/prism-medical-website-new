Common Questions

Compression Therapy FAQ

How often should compression garments be replaced?
Most medical-grade garments should be replaced about every six months, or sooner with daily wear. We track your schedule and contact you before replacement is due.
What compression class do I need?
Your clinician prescribes the compression class based on your condition. We supply exactly what's prescribed and help with sizing so it's effective and wearable.
Are compression garments covered?
Coverage varies by diagnosis and plan. Lymphedema-related compression is covered under the LTA for Medicare patients; we verify your specific benefits before ordering.
